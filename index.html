AeroParts Commerce Hub

Validated end-to-end purchasing, quote, checkout, pricing, availability, and integration workflows across a connected enterprise commerce ecosystem.

The platform relied on search services, APIs, Salesforce business rules, and ERP-backed data sources. My role involved validating customer-facing functionality while identifying which system layer was responsible when issues occurred.

End-to-EndPostmanSalesforceSAPPlaywright Automation

Key responsibilities

  • Tested product discovery, product details, personalized pricing, availability, delivery information, cart, checkout, ordering, and quote workflows.
  • Validated data flow between the storefront, search/BFF services, APIs, Salesforce business logic, and ERP systems.
  • Verified customer permissions, checkout rules, notification workflows, and account-specific functionality.
  • Executed end-to-end, regression, exploratory, and integration testing across customer purchasing journeys.
  • Investigated defects across UI, Search/BFF, Salesforce, API, and SAP layers.
  • Validated integrations using Postman by reviewing request and response payloads, status codes, business rules, data accuracy, and downstream behavior.
  • Created and executed test cases to validate business requirements and reduce regression risk before release.
